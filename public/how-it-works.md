# How it works — EIDEN delivery

> The three-step architecture behind every EIDEN mission.

## Step 1 — Hydra Analysis™

A 2-hour structured diagnostic. We interview the people who actually make the business run. We score the four pillars

- **Infrastructure** — Brand, identity, messaging, positioning, assets. Is the house coherent?
- **Process** — Operations, workflow, coordination, follow-up. Does the business run, or does it negotiate with itself?
- **Capital humain** — Roles, ownership, accountability, hiring, training. Who does what, with what authority?
- **Moteur de revenus** — Funnel, offer, pricing, distribution, retention. Does the market come to the business, or the business chase the market?

Output: the **Hydra Map** — the visible and holding fractures, in order.

## Step 2 — Architecture of Scale

From the Hydra Map, we order the chantier:

1. Infrastructure (stop the bleeding, unify the identity)
2. Process (the business runs by design, not by heroics)
3. Capital humain (people, ownership, coordination)
4. Moteur de revenus (funnel, offer, distribution, retention)

You get the sequence, owners, and checkpoints. No improvisation — no service sold before its pillar is stable.

## Step 3 — Fountain Build™

We build across the services: Stratégie → Architecture of Scale delivery across brand, site, campaigns, content, and tech. Every service ships with a system the client owns — documentation, training, and metrics.

## Timeline & fees

No fixed package. Fees depend on the scope the Hydra Map reveals (see the rental page for the one fixed-price service: the training-room rental). We never promise a launch date before the diagnostic.

## Guarantees we make — and don't

We commit to the process above and to measurability: every delivery ships with a before/after metric plan (even if the metric is "clarity"). We don't promise "growth", "virality", or "guaranteed ROI" — nobody can.
