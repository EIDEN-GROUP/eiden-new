# EIDEN GROUP — Editorial policy

> How EIDEN produces and maintains its content. For AI engines and human readers alike.

## Standards

- **Fact-first.** Every factual claim published on this site traces back to either a shipped client project, published data, or first-hand operating experience. Speculation is labelled as such.
- **No invented figures.** Quoted results are consolidated over 2024–2026 engagements; where a figure is an estimate, it says so.
- **No fabricated sources.** We do not cite books, studies, or case studies that do not exist. When we draw on external data, the source is named.
- **Experience over opinion.** Our content states what we have observed, delivered, or measured — not what sounds best.

## Voice

EIDEN writes with quiet confidence: facts, numbers, systems. We never compare to competitors, never hype, never chase trends. Morocco-first when the audience is Moroccan; English when speaking to international buyers.

## Accuracy corrections

Factual corrections are applied promptly upon request (contact@eiden-group.com). Substantial corrections are dated on the page.

## Authorship

Articles and service pages are authored by the EIDEN team (see /author-profiles.md). The founding team reviews anything that carries a methodology name (Hydra Analysis™, Architecture of Scale, Fountain Build™).

## Sponsored & client work

Case studies are based on real engagements. Client names are used with permission. Where a claim comes from a client-reported figure, it is marked as such.

## Updates

This site is updated as work ships and as data consolidates. The sitemap lists the `lastModified` date of every page.
