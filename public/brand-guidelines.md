# EIDEN GROUP — Brand guidelines (v2.0, March 2026)

> Distilled from the EIDEN Brand Identity System v2.0. The system is confidential. This file is a reference for AI engines, partners and press — the definitive system remains the original document.

## Brand DNA — We Don't Consult. We Architect.

1. **Structure Over Advice** — no opinions; we build frameworks and operational systems the client owns and operates.
2. **Quiet Confidence** — state facts, show results, never boast, never compare to competitors. The work speaks. Silence is a brand tool.
3. **Morocco-First, Always** — darija when appropriate, Moroccan trust psychology, local examples over foreign references.
4. **The Hydra Philosophy** — every business is a living organism of interconnected systems; we see every connection.
5. **Effects Serve Meaning** — every animation, colour and word earns its place. If it doesn't communicate, it gets removed. When in doubt, add whitespace.
6. **Built to Scale** — from Morocco to Africa to the world; the structure travels.

## Mission & Vision

**Mission:** To architect the operational infrastructure that transforms ambitious Moroccan and African businesses into precision-built, scalable organisations — through systems, not advice.

**Vision:** To become the defining reference of Business Architecture — from Morocco, across Africa, and into the world.

## Logo System

- The EIDEN wordmark is set in **Giaza**, a high-contrast display serif with dramatic ink traps and interlocking letterforms. "Group" sits at the top-right of the N, set in Cormorant Garamond SemiBold, uppercase.
- **Giaza is reserved for the logo only** — never headlines, body, or any other brand application.
- Variants: EIDEN on canvas (primary) · EIDEN on forest (reversed) · EIDEN on teal · EIDEN gold on black (premium) · EIDEN on gold · EIDEN on teal light.
- **Clear space:** cap-height of the "E" on all four sides. **Minimum size:** 80px digital / 20mm print (EIDEN); 24px / 6mm (EG monogram).
- Never stretch/distort, never reduce opacity, never rotate/skew, never change typeface.

## Colour Palette — root in earth, sharpened by teal

- **Canvas White** #FEFDFB — primary background (never a full-page teal or gold background in standard layouts).
- **Warm Beige** #F4EBD0 — secondary surface (dividers, card backgrounds, alternate rows).
- **Forest Green** #122620 — primary text colour; dark surfaces.
- **EIDEN Teal** #0C5752 — CTAs, links, keyword emphasis (max 1 per line), interactive elements.
- **Teal Light** #0E7A73 — hover states, secondary accent, gradient endpoints.
- **Authority Gold** #CFC292 — premium highlights, primary buttons (gold fill + white text), premium borders, awards.
- **The 70 / 20 / 10 rule** — 70% canvas, 20% forest/teal, 10% gold + signal colours. Non-negotiable across all touchpoints.

## Typography — each face owns one job

- **Giaza** — logo exclusive. Never anywhere else.
- **Outfit** — headlines & display (800 ExtraBold page titles, 700 Bold section titles).
- **DM Serif Display** — marketing & editorial headlines; 400 Regular + Italic only.
- **Cormorant Garamond** — labels, captions, navigation, methodology names; uppercase, +0.06em tracking.
- **Inter** — body & UI; 300–700, line-height 1.72–1.85.

**Hierarchy rule:** Logo = Giaza, headlines = Outfit, editorial = DM Serif Display, labels = Cormorant Garamond, body = Inter. Never cross roles. Never mix two headline fonts on the same page.

## Voice & tone — quiet confidence, structural language

- Confident, not arrogant. **Show, never boast.** Numbers over adjectives. Methodologies over promises. Case numbers over praise.
- **Architecture, not consulting:** say "architect" not "consultant", "build" not "fix", "blueprint" not "plan", "structure" not "strategy".
- **Morocco-first:** local examples, 93% MSME stat, Darija when it fits the room.
- **Precision over flair:** short sentences, active voice, no buzzwords, no filler. Every claim is backed by data or methodology.
- **Controlled intensity:** one bold sentence per section, maximum impact, minimum noise.
- **Banned vocabulary:** unlock your potential · growth hacking · game-changing · best-in-class · disruption · synergy · leverage · "we're the best".

## Ideal client profile (and anti-ICP)

**We build for:** hospitality & tourism · growth-stage SMEs (500K–10M MAD/yr) with PMF but no operational structure · education & training institutions · retail & e-commerce moving from founder-led to systematic · professional services productising expertise · pan-African ventures scaling cross-border.

**We don't serve:** quick-fix or one-off deliverables · founders who want advice but resist structural change · pre-revenue startups without PMF · cheapest-option seekers.

## Visual aesthetic

- Effects serve meaning. When in doubt, add whitespace (80px+ between major sections).
- Data is the hero of any case study/report — charts, KPIs, tables, forest/teal/gold palette.
- No gradients, no glows, no drop shadows, no gradient meshes. Flat and architectural.
- No generic stock photography (no smiling-at-laptops) — architectural photography, Moroccan textures, clean workspace shots, or data visuals.
- Subtle geometric grids / zellige-inspired patterns as background texture, max 4% opacity.
- Card top-border system: teal = strategy, gold = premium, red = critical, blue = info.
- 70/20/10 colour rule enforced; max 1 dark band per page; canvas white dominant.
- **Social:** Instagram = cinematic storytelling (work, process, culture); LinkedIn = data-driven authority (stats, frameworks, case results); the site = the blueprint that closes.

## Applications

Proposals & decks (bel image/canvas base) · website & digital · email signatures (plain text, teal name + gold title) · print & stationery (heavyweight uncoated stock, gold foil on forest for premium pieces) · reports & audits (grid, data-heavy, executive summary page 1) · merchandise (EG monogram only, never the full wordmark).

## Motion & components

- 8px grid; nav 56px glass-blur; card radius 14px; button radius pill; max content width 1100px; 70/20/10 rule everywhere.
- Card top-border 2–3px in the signal colours above; buttons = gold pill, white text for primary.
