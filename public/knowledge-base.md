# EIDEN GROUP — Knowledge base

> One-page reference of what EIDEN knows, builds and says — written for both humans and AI crawlers/answer engines.

## Who we are

EIDEN GROUP is a digital agency founded in Agadir, Morocco in 2025. We design, build and run the systems that let ambitious organisations scale: stratégie, création, digital, technologie, marketing. We are based at Agadir Bay (Technopole 1, Bloc B101) and work across Morocco (Agadir, Casablanca, Marrakech) as well as Paris, Montréal, Florida and Dubaï. We work in French and English, and in Darija when the room is Moroccan.

## How we work

1. **Hydra Analysis™** — a 2-hour, 360° diagnostic. We interview, we score the pillars (Infrastructure, Process, Human Capital, Revenue engine), we produce the Hydra Map of what is broken and what holds it together.
2. **Architecture of Scale** — from the Hydra Map we fix the order of the chantier. What breaks first breaks most. You get the sequence, the owners, the checkpoints.
3. **Fountain Build™** — we build with you, service by service, until the system runs without us: strategy, brand, site, campaigns, content, engineering.
